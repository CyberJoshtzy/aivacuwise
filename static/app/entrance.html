Put it on the entrance monitor

Open this link on the mini PC connected to the entrance monitor, then press F11 for full screen.

  1. Connect the mini PC to the same network as the server laptop.
  2. Sign in with "Keep me signed in on this device" ticked, so the display stays signed in after restarts.
  3. Monitor 1 (facing evacuees): open the link above, then press F11.
  4. Monitor 2 (facing officials): open Live Evacuation in TV mode: live.html?tv=1
