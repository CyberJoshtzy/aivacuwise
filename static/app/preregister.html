Pre-Registration

Register an evacuee before a disaster so the entrance camera can recognize them at any evacuation center.

    Step 1: Capture face photos

    Camera off
    1. Left side
    2. Front
    3. Right side
    Three photos will be taken: left side, front, and right side
    Ask the evacuee to remove caps, sunglasses, and face masks, and sit about an arm's length from the laptop. Your browser will ask permission to use the camera.

    Captured photos (0 of 3)

    Infants do not need face photos. A baby's face changes quickly and is usually not visible while being carried, so the camera cannot match it reliably. The infant will be linked to a parent or guardian and checked in automatically when that person is recognized. You may take one front photo so staff can identify the infant on the Entrance Display.

    For side photos, turn the head only about 30 to 45 degrees so both eyes stay visible. A full side view cannot be matched by the camera.
