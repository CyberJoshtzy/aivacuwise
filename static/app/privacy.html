Data Privacy Notice and Confidentiality Agreement

Please read before continuing to the dashboard.

Ai-Vacuwise processes personal and sensitive personal information of evacuees, including facial recognition data, in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Information in this system

Full name, birthdate, sex, address, contact number, household membership, facial recognition data, check-in records, and tracker locations.

Why it is collected

To identify evacuees at evacuation centers, monitor center occupancy, and help reunite families during disasters.

Your responsibilities as an authorized official

  • Access evacuee records only for disaster response duties.
  • Do not copy, photograph, print, or share evacuee data outside this system.
  • Collect each evacuee's written consent before registering their face.
  • Sign out whenever you leave the device.
  • Report any suspected data breach to the City Hall IT Administrator immediately.

Storage and retention

Data is stored on the barangay's local server. It is not sold or shared with third parties, and it is copied only to the city's secured server for City Hall monitoring.

  • Evacuation, check-in and relief records are kept for three (3) years after the evacuation ends, for reporting and audit by the City DRRM Office, then securely deleted.
  • Facial recognition data and resident profiles are kept while the resident is registered in the barangay. They are deleted within thirty (30) days after the resident moves out or asks for deletion.
  • Tracker location data is deleted thirty (30) days after the evacuation ends.
