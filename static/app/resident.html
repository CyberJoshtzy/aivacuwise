When you evacuate

  1. Bring IDs, medicine, water, a flashlight and a charged phone.
  2. Go to the evacuation center your barangay names.
  3. Walk slowly past the entrance camera and face it. You are checked in automatically.
  4. Keep your GPS tracker on you, if you have one.
  5. Tell the barangay staff before you leave the center.
