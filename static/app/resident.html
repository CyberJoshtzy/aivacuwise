    ⚠What to do when evacuating

    • Walk slowly past the entrance camera and face it so you are checked in automatically.
    • Keep your GPS tracker on and with you at all times.
    • Bring IDs, medicine, water, a flashlight and a charged phone.
    • Do not leave the evacuation center without telling the barangay staff.
    • For emergencies, press the Emergency Hotlines button.
